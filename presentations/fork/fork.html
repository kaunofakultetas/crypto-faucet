Blockchain forks

Roughly every ten minutes a miner somewhere finds the next block. Now and then two miners find one at almost the same moment, and for a few minutes the network cannot agree which chain is the real one.

Step through to see how a fork happens, how it heals itself, and why the rule that heals it is what makes Bitcoin work.
